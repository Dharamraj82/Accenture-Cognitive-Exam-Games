How to Play Directional Doors & Grid Maze

👤

Move the Player: Use Arrow Keys, W A S D, click adjacent cells, or use the on-screen D-Pad.

🔑

Collect All Keys: You must grab every key on the board before the exit door unlocks.

🚪

Reach the Door: Once all keys are collected, step onto the door to finish the stage!

🧱

Beware of Invisible Walls: Hidden obstacles block certain paths. Colliding with one reveals it briefly and resets you back to start!

🎲

Full Procedural Randomness: Every stage generates a fresh, unique, solvable layout every time you play.

💡

Solution Helper: Stuck? Click Show Solution Path to view the optimal BFS route.
